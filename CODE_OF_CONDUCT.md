## 简体中文

### 我们的承诺

为了营造开放友好的环境，我们作为贡献者和维护者承诺：让每个人参与本项目和社区的体验不受骚扰，无论年龄、体型、残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向。

### 我们的标准

有助于营造积极环境的行为包括：

- 使用友善和包容的语言
- 尊重不同的观点和经历
- 平和地接受建设性批评
- 以社区的最大利益为出发点
- 对其他社区成员保持同理心

不可接受的行为包括：

- 使用性化的语言或图像，以及不受欢迎的性关注
- 挑衅、侮辱性评论以及人身或政治攻击
- 公开或私下骚扰
- 未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息
- 其他在专业场合中合理认定为不当的行为

### 执行

如遇辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，可通过 **hyhmrright@gmail.com** 举报。所有投诉都将被审查与调查，并作出适当回应。维护者有权移除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、issue 等。

本准则改编自 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) v2.1。

## English

### Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

### Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to **hyhmrright@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate. Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
